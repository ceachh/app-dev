-*The [Rio franchise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_(2011_film)) is a highly successful animated media series set in a vibrant, music-filled world where animals find their voice amid dazzling tropical landscapes. Originally produced by Blue Sky Studios and distributed by 20th Century Fox, the mainline story tracks the dangerous adventures, cultural awakening, and personal growth of a neurotic, flightless, and highly domesticated Spix's macaw named Blu.*

## Main Characters
1. <img width="50" height="50" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/74202e95-a735-4c54-b238-5eec718b6149" />
 Blu (Jesse Eisenberg) – A highly intelligent, socially awkward blue macaw who grew up as a domesticated pet in Minnesota. After traveling to Brazil, he must learn to overcome his fears, embrace his wild side, and protect his family.

2. <img width="50" height="50" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a39d61b1-758d-4617-8167-ff3fbbc8c023" />

3. <img width="50" height="50" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/49814e0a-1b75-4ab9-a694-92906df8a270" />
